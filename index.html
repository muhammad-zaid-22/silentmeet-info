1. Introduction

SilentMeet (also distributed as "Meeting Silent") is developed by Ailab Solutions ("we", "us", "our"). The app automatically silences your phone based on location-based Silent Zones and scheduled or prayer-time reminders, so you are never interrupted where silence matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, why, and the choices you have.

We built SilentMeet to be privacy-first and offline-capable. The core silencing engine runs on your device, and the majority of your settings never leave your phone.

2. Information We Collect

  • Location data — used to detect when you enter or leave your Silent Zones (see Section 3).
  • Account information (optional) — if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo through Google / Firebase Authentication. Signing in is optional; the app can be used as a guest.
  • App preferences and content you create — your Silent Zones, schedules, sound/volume profiles, selected language, faith profile, and similar settings.
  • Subscription status — whether you are on a free trial, guest, or premium plan, so your entitlement is honored across reinstalls.
  • Approximate diagnostic and device info — such as operating-system version and coarse region, used to apply the correct prayer-time calculation method and to keep the app stable.

We do not collect your contacts, call logs, messages, photos, or microphone audio. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Location Data & Geofencing

Background location disclosure SilentMeet uses location data — including in the background — solely to detect when you enter or exit a Silent Zone you have created, so it can automatically silence or restore your phone. Background location is never used for advertising, tracking, or profiling.

Location is central to the app's purpose:

  • Foreground location lets you place a Silent Zone on the map when adding a location.
  • Background ("Allow all the time") location lets the geofencing engine trigger auto-silence even when the app is closed. Without it, zone-based silencing cannot run while the app is in the background.
  • Your coordinates are processed on-device to compute zone entry/exit and to calculate accurate prayer times for your area. Your continuous location history is not transmitted to us or stored on our servers.

You may deny or revoke location access at any time in your device settings. Location-based features will simply stop working; the rest of the app continues to function.

4. Local Storage on Your Device

Most of your data lives only on your phone, in the app's private local storage. This includes your Silent Zones, schedules, sound profiles, language and faith preferences, and permission choices. This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

If you sign in, a minimal set of data (such as your subscription status and faith identity) may be stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so your plan and preferences can be restored if you reinstall or switch devices.

5. Device Permissions We Request

SilentMeet requests permissions contextually — only when a feature needs them — and explains why beforehand:

📍 Location

Detect Silent Zone entry/exit and compute local prayer times.

🔔 Notifications

Show silence status, reminders, and prayer/schedule alerts.

🌙 Do Not Disturb access

Silence and restore your ringer/DND state automatically.

⏰ Exact alarms

Fire prayer and scheduled reminders at the precise time.

🔋 Battery optimization

Allow reliable background silencing without the OS killing the service.

🔄 Run at startup

Re-arm your zones and schedules after a device restart.

6. How We Use Information

  • To automatically silence and restore your phone in your Silent Zones and on your schedules.
  • To calculate and deliver accurate prayer times and reminders for your region.
  • To authenticate you (if you sign in) and to honor your subscription across devices and reinstalls.
  • To keep the app secure, stable, and functioning as intended.

7. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Limited data is processed by trusted providers strictly to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore) — optional sign-in and subscription/preference sync.
  • Google Play Services & Google Maps — map display, location services, and geofencing on Android.
  • Google Play Billing — if you purchase a subscription, payment is handled by Google; we do not receive your card details.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We share information only as needed to provide the service or where required by law.

8. Data Retention & Security

On-device data is retained until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall. Any cloud-synced data (for signed-in users) is retained while your account is active and deleted on request. We rely on the security of Google's infrastructure and Android's app-sandboxing to protect your information, and we limit collection to what the features genuinely require.

9. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & control — view and edit your zones, schedules, and preferences at any time in the app.
  • Revoke permissions — turn off location, notifications, or DND access in your device settings whenever you like.
  • Delete your data — clear the app's storage or uninstall to remove local data; contact us to delete any cloud-synced account data.
  • Use as a guest — you can use core features without creating an account.

10. Children's Privacy

SilentMeet is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us and we will remove it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
